## Atkore's peers, by product

**Why a chosen set.** The SEC files Atkore Inc. under SIC 3690, Miscellaneous Electrical Machinery, Equipment & Supplies. EDGAR's company
list for that code is mostly battery and EV-charger makers (docs/source-audits/studio-online-data-and-tools.md §5.1),
so a set built on the code would compare Atkore with them.

**What Atkore makes**, from its [10-K 0001628280-25-054049](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1666138/000162828025054049/atkr-20250930.htm), for the year to 2025-09-30:

- Electrical: "Significant product categories within our Electrical segment include metal electrical conduit and fittings, plastic pipe conduit and fittings, electrical cable and flexible conduit, and international cable management systems"
- Safety & Infrastructure: "Significant product categories within our Safety & Infrastructure segment include mechanical tube, metal framing & fittings, construction services and perimeter security"

The industry view sums this up as "including conduit, fittings, electrical cable, cable management, metal framing, mechanical tube". Each of those words is checked against the passages above.

**The competitors it names**, read from the same report:

- Electrical: Zekelman Industries, Inc.; Mitsubishi Corporation; Nucor Corporation; Southwire Company, LLC; Dura-Line Corporation; Prysmian
- Safety & Infrastructure: Zekelman Industries, Inc.; Eaton Corporation plc; ABB Ltd.; Hubbell Incorporated; nVent Electric plc.; Haydon Corporation

Every one of them is either in the set or on the missing list below. The script refuses to call a set
clean otherwise.

problem, and Studio hides the set until the quotes are reviewed in the manifest and the script is run again.
