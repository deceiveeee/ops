## Objective

Build an optional investigation framework for the 22 Damodaran sessions outside the
16-session required mission backbone. Every investigation asks one question:

> Does this claimed edge survive the learner's Friction Budget, Evidence Checklist, and
> Architecture/Edge License?

The labs deepen research skill. They never become hidden prerequisites, owned holdings,
automatic active licenses, or required progress steps.

## Shared investigation rhythm

Each lab uses a common reasoning protocol but a concept-native visual:

1. **Claim:** state the alleged edge precisely.
2. **Mechanism:** who is wrong, why, and what corrects the mispricing?
3. **Historical specimen:** inspect the source-era evidence with date and limitations.
4. **Autopsy:** identify benchmark, risk model, sample, survivorship, data-mining, causality,
   holdout, and execution problems.
5. **Friction checkout:** apply the learner's saved Mission 8 budget.
6. **Net decision:** reject, quarantine, or send a research-only candidate to the Mission 10
   Edge License.
7. **Falsifier:** state what evidence ends the investigation.
8. **Independent transfer:** test an unfamiliar claim from the same family without hints.

The lab may save a research note, rejected claim, unanswered question, or Edge License
candidate. It cannot directly alter portfolio weights or holdings.

## Family-specific visual direction

- Momentum: horizon/decay scanner showing signal definition, crowding, turnover, and
  out-of-sample decay—not a decorative price chart.
- Value: four-stage cheapness → risk → growth → reinvestment/quality screen plus a myth
  check; correct the known malformed P/B expression before any assessment.
- Growth: growth/reinvestment bridge showing when growth creates versus destroys value.
- Information: expectations/news-quality monitor linking surprise, cash-flow consequence,
  source provenance, and legal/dated boundaries.
- Arbitrage: cash-flow replication and convergence-risk scanner; show funding, execution,
  model, and survival risks rather than promising riskless profit.

## Progress and architecture rules

- Labs are marked optional and never count toward 13-mission completion or graduation.
- The course map keeps Missions as the only core direction.
- Lab state is separate from required checkpoint state.
- A completed lab does not grant Architecture licensed.
- A lab candidate must still pass the current Mission 10 gate, with Mission 5 sizing and
  Mission 7/8/9 evidence.
- Changing friction/evidence/architecture marks any dependent investigation conclusion for
  review without blocking an otherwise passive core portfolio.
- Learners can skip every lab and graduate with full competence in the required course.
