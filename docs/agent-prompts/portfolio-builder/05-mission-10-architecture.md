## Source and calculation boundaries

Preserve the audit's exact qualifications:

- passive is a defensible default, not proof nobody can win;
- current success rates vary by category, horizon, cost, and survivorship method;
- all current numbers are dated to their data period and high-decay;
- historical winners can arise from luck;
- a streak is not evidence of durable skill;
- complexity is not an edge;
- market efficiency is market-, investor-, information-, and cost-specific;
- apparent alpha is jointly dependent on the strategy, benchmark/risk model, and estimates;
- gross edge must clear the saved Friction Budget;
- Sharpe is based on differential/excess return and does not capture portfolio correlation;
- exact calculations and source quiz defects in the audit control over published errors.

Never turn Session 35 locality/concentration anecdotes into beginner sizing advice.

## Learner experience

### Architecture Switchboard

Start with the learner's Mission 5 strategic sleeves. Show the passive implementation path
as enabled by default. An active candidate begins disabled.

For each proposed active sleeve, require:

- exposure it replaces and the correct benchmark;
- specific market pocket/inefficiency;
- who or what is wrong and why;
- correction mechanism and expected horizon;
- recognition/execution capability;
- falsifiable claim and disconfirming evidence;
- current category base rate with date and limitations;
- evidence design, holdout, survivor-safe sample, and risk model;
- gross expected edge;
- saved fees, turnover, spread, tax-warning, and capacity costs;
- net edge range rather than false precision;
- maximum allocation and portfolio loss contribution inherited from Mission 5;
- durability and imitation risk;
- thesis-break condition and review date.

No single field or impressive number may unlock the sleeve. The UI names every unmet
condition keeping it disabled.

### Visual metaphor

Use an **Edge License / architecture switchboard**. The strongest visual is the route from
claim through gates to portfolio expression. Show leakage from gross to net edge and the
consequence for the actual portfolio. Avoid a generic checklist grid.

### Learning sequence

1. Define passive, active, benchmark, base rate, persistence, null, capacity, durability,
   and net edge.
2. Model a complete proposal that looks persuasive but fails after risk/friction.
3. Guided practice tests one field at a time and reveals why the sleeve stays disabled.
4. Learner applies the license to their watchlist or a supplied practice candidate.
5. Independent perturbation introduces an unfamiliar winning streak/current proposal.
6. Assessment requires a justified enable/disable decision and identifies evidence that
   would reverse it.

An active sleeve is optional. A fully passive portfolio can demonstrate mastery.

## State and completion

Mission 10 may set **Architecture licensed** only when:

- allocation, watchlist/value, friction, and evidence prerequisites are valid;
- passive/core exposure and benchmark are defined;
- any active sleeve clears every required field;
- sizing respects the Mission 5 loss budget;
- a failed proposal remains visibly disabled;
- learner passes the unfamiliar proposal assessment;
- source dates and assumption ownership are stored.

Changing friction, evidence, valuation, allocation, or a high-decay source date later marks
the Edge License and dependent timing, product slate, overlap, order, flight-test,
operating, and IPS records `Review required`.
