# Portfolio Builder curriculum approval

**Approval date:** 2026-08-12

**Decision:** approved by the stakeholder in the active OPS curriculum review.

## Approved authority

The authoritative Portfolio Builder direction is:

- **13 required missions**, not a competing mission/module structure;
- **build while learning**: every mission changes the same persistent Portfolio Workbench;
- **completed portfolio at graduation**: the learner finishes with a complete personal
  portfolio when appropriate, or an equally assessed paper portfolio and deployment plan;
- **two equal modes** from Mission 1: Build mine and Practice case;
- **controlled state progression**:
  `mandate → strategic weights → research-only watchlist → architecture license → timing
  policy → product slate → order rehearsal → operating plan`;
- **no ownership shortcut**: Missions 6–7 create research candidates only; exact holdings
  enter in Mission 12 after friction, evidence, and architecture gates;
- **competence, not completion clicks**: graduation requires the completed Dossier/IPS, an
  unfamiliar transfer case, and no critical safety failure;
- **all 38 Damodaran sessions preserved** as required-session support or optional edge labs,
  rather than 38 learner-facing progress steps;
- an optional, competition-neutral **Investment Committee Studio** may later extend the
  curriculum with team governance, industry research, attribution, reporting, and oral
  defense. It must not be marketed as Wharton Competition preparation under the current
  Wharton external-course restriction without written organizer permission.

## What this approval does not mean

Curriculum approval closes the stakeholder architecture gate. It does **not**:

- mark a planned mission as implemented;
- close an identified primary-source or narration gap;
- approve a numerical threshold merely because it appears in a design document;
- replace the required claim/prerequisite/practice/assessment matrix for each mission;
- waive finance verification, accessibility, responsive, theme, browser, or visual QA;
- authorize live trades, brokerage integration, personalized advice, or a professional
  credential claim;
- guarantee success in any investment competition.

Every mission must still pass the ordered OPS Gate A–E workflow before release.
